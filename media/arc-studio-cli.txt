What this shows
- The project id 430cf754-291a-4e76-a84b-603ffc815002 is the same id in the workspace URL
  https://studio.arc.io/app/430cf754-291a-4e76-a84b-603ffc815002. The contracts, their tests and
  the front end were written inside that workspace.
- Studio's own deployment record is the Arc Testnet contract 0x004ea6ef1fba77b71fc74e47e525ed7d92a0193b.
  Studio builds and tests, and it does not deploy to mainnet on your behalf, which is why the
  mainnet contract in the README (0xaa1ad37719722a5dce26439595f9ea2e532a56c2) was deployed
  separately with Foundry and the project's own key.
- The mainnet transactions are listed in README.md and their raw receipts are in media/receipts.json,
  read from https://rpc.mainnet.arc.io so they can be checked without a block explorer login.
